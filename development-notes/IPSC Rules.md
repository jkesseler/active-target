[#ELECTRONIC SCORING TARGETS LIST](https://www.ipsc.org/electronic-scoring-targets-list/)
The target must be capable of registering subsequent hits with a time span of 0.09 second minimum.


## Target Types
See Rullbook appendix B and C

## Names:
### "Target"
3 zone target`A`, `B` and `D`.
Scoring A: 5, B: 2, S: 1
No-Score: -

## "Micro Target"
3 zone target`A`, `B` and `D`.
Scoring A: 5, B: 2, D: 1
No-Score: -

### "Popper": 
Single zone
Scoring:  5 points
No-Score -10 points (penalty)

### "Mini Popper": 
Single zone
Scoring:  5 points
No-Score -10 points (penalty)

### "Falling Plate" 
Single zone
Scoring:  5 points
No-Score -10 points (penalty)


### "Stop Plate"
Ends the stage
Must be clearly distinguishable from regular plates and contain visual electronic
indicators to confirm it has been hit
